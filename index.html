About

Building Data Platforms & Intelligent Applications

I build data platforms, machine learning systems, and AI-powered applications that transform raw data into reliable pipelines, actionable insights, and intelligent solutions.

I am Abdul Ahad, a final-year Computer Science student at the University of Engineering & Technology (UET), Lahore, and a Microsoft Certified: Fabric Data Engineer Associate. I focus on Data Engineering, Modern Data Platforms, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.

I work with technologies including Microsoft Fabric, PySpark, Lakehouses, Python, SQL, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, FastAPI, and Power BI.

I enjoy connecting modern data engineering with AI and analytics to build practical systems that turn data into measurable insights and intelligent solutions.
